About Sam

Samuel Fleming writes science fiction and fantasy that explore survival and adaptation, and occasionally horror. A background in English, and in a past life a lifeguard and pool manager. When not writing, Sam is exploring other worlds through reading or gaming: lately Cradle, Dungeon Crawler Carl and Children of Time.
